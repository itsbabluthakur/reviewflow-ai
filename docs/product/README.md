# Product Documentation

## Purpose

Supporting product research and context that informs — but is not part of — the authoritative root [`PRD.md`](../../PRD.md).

## What belongs here

* Customer/persona research and interview notes.
* Competitive analysis.
* Pricing and packaging exploration that hasn't yet been finalized into `PRD.md`.

## What should NOT be placed here

* The finalized product requirements, MVP scope, or roadmap phases — those live in root `PRD.md` and `ROADMAP.md`.
